# Hi, I'm Abdellahi , welcome to my blog.

I'm using this space to publish write-ups and notes from HackTheBox and TryHackMe machines, CTF challenges, and the security projects I build, including [CloudGuard](https://github.com/abdallahi34/cloudguard), a cloud security detection pipeline covering IaC scanning, posture monitoring, and CloudTrail-based detection mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.

## What to expect

- Machine and CTF write-ups, with a focus on *why* an exploit works, not just the steps to reproduce it
- Detection engineering notes , Sigma rules, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, what makes a detection rule noisy versus reliable
- Cloud security notes, tied to what I'm building in CloudGuard
- Occasional short posts on tools, labs, or lessons learned

## A note on write-ups

Machine write-ups here are only published once the machine is retired, in line with HackTheBox's rules on active machines.
